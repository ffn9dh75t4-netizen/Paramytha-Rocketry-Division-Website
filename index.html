What we do

At Rocket Propulsion Laboratories, we focus on the fascinating world of sugar-propellant rocketry. Our work involves exploring solid-propellant rocket technology, understanding how rocket propulsion works, and developing our knowledge of aerospace engineering.

We are passionate about designing rocket projects, studying propulsion systems, and learning through experimentation and supervised testing.

From initial concepts to launch-day observations, we aim to push our understanding of rocketry further, develop our engineering skills, and turn our curiosity into innovation.

Our goal is simple: Learn, innovate and reach new heights.

About Us

Rocket Propulsion Laboratories (RPL)

Rocket Propulsion Laboratories (RPL) is an independent rocketry initiative driven by curiosity, engineering and a passion for aerospace. We aim to explore the science behind rockets, develop practical skills and gain a deeper understanding of how things work beyond the ground.

At RPL, we design, build and develop model rockets, investigate different propulsion concepts and study the engineering behind rocket flight. Our projects include researching rocket designs, constructing airframes, examining propulsion technologies, testing components and documenting our results.

We also share our projects, experiments and discoveries, giving others an insight into our work and the science behind rocketry.
