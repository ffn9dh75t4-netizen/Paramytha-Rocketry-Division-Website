About Us

Paramytha Rocketry Division

Paramytha Rocketry Division is a rocketry organisation driven by curiosity, innovation and a passion for aerospace engineering.

Our goal is to explore the world of rocketry through designing, developing and testing rocket projects. We believe in learning through experimentation, creativity and teamwork.
